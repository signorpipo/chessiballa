# Known nights and sources (seed list)

Starting knowledge for the Researcher, collected by hand on 2026-11-26. It is a hint list,
not a limit: the agent should still look for nights that are not here. Later the list is
meant to grow on its own between runs (ELIA-23).

"Readable" means a plain fetch without JavaScript or login returns the content, which is
what `web_fetch` can use.

## Area

Milan and the nearby hinterland only (for example Sesto San Giovanni, Corsico). Events in
other provinces are out, even when a Milan organiser runs them (e.g. Toilet Camp in
Sorisole, BG).

## Organisers that move between venues

The Original / Enjoy Together (themed nights at Hot Dog and Depot), Company Club (bear
events), Il Padre XXL, COX, Queer Events Milano (RebelNight, SuperQueens, Queer House),
Toilet Club (specials at Black Hole, Base Milano). Search for their names together with
"Milano" and the month.

## Sources that do not work for the pipeline

- Blocked (403): misterbandb.com, ra.co, travelgay.com, gayout.com (Cloudflare check),
  gay.it, milanotoday.it, dice.fm, gaycities.com. Their text may still appear in search
  snippets; treat snippets as weak evidence.

- JavaScript-only: ticketsms.it, taplink.cc, companyclub.org, ilpadrexxl.com.
- Broken: metroclubmilano.it and tbluesauna.it (TLS errors).

## Out of scope

- Nights for queer women only (Eva+Eva, Butterfly, L-EX).
- Club privé for couples and mixed crowds (Nautilus).
- Drag shows, pageants or theatre without dancing (e.g. Exotic Queen Italy).
- Anything outside the area above (e.g. Gothicsauna in Lugano).
